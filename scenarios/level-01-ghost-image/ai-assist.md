## AI tip: paste the evidence, not your summary

The AI only knows what you paste. A one-line symptom gets you a generic checklist.

🚫 **Bad:** "My pod is stuck, what do I do?"

✅ **Good:** paste the **whole** Events section of the failing pod, then ask:
"Quote the line that says WHY it failed. Is it an auth, network or 'does not exist' problem,
and what evidence separates them?"

**Check before you trust it:**
- Ask the AI how you can *verify* any value it suggests. Do not accept a value it cannot back with evidence.
- Treat the answer as a hypothesis. Confirm it in the cluster before you change anything.
- Never paste secrets or real internal hostnames.
